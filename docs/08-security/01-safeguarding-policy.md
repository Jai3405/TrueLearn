## 1. For students — please read this bit

**True Learn is a maths tutor. It is a computer program, not a person.** It will never
pretend to be your friend, your counsellor, or a human being.

### I am not a secret

If you tell True Learn that **someone is hurting you**, or that **you might hurt yourself**,
**a real person at our company will read that part of your conversation.** They will do
something about it.

We are telling you this **before** you use it, not afterwards, because you deserve to know
the rules before you decide what to say.

### What we will not do

- We will **not** close the app on you or stop talking to you.
- We will **not** tell you off, lecture you, or say you were wrong to tell us.
- We will **not** share your maths mistakes, your marks, or your ordinary conversations with
  your parents or teachers. Those are yours.

### What we will do

- Show you people you can talk to **right now**, day or night — see §5.
- Ask a trained adult at our company to read what you wrote, usually **within an hour**.
- Depending on what you told us, we may contact **your parent or guardian**, someone at
  **your school**, or **the police**.

**If the person hurting you is at home, we will not contact your home.** That is a rule we
have written down and built into the software, not a promise invented on the spot.

### You can ask us what happened

Email **[DCPO EMAIL — `FD-08`]** and ask what we did and who we told. We will tell you
honestly.

## 2. What happens, depending on what you tell us

The plain version of `ADR-011`.

| What you say | What we do |
|---|---|
| **You are feeling low or stressed, or someone is being cruel to you** | We show you support numbers. Someone reviews it, and we usually tell your school counsellor the next working day — a short summary, not your whole conversation |
| **You talk about hurting yourself, or about not wanting to be alive** | Support numbers straight away. A trained person reviews it within an hour, at any time of day or night. We will normally contact your parent or guardian. If we think you are in immediate danger and cannot reach them, we will call **112** |
| **Someone is hurting you, or touching you in a way you do not want** | A named person at our company reports it to the police or the Special Juvenile Police Unit. **This is the law in India and we do not get to decide otherwise.** We do not investigate first. **If that person is in your home, we do not tell your home** |
| **You talk about seriously hurting someone else** | A named person reviews it and contacts the police |

**Why we report abuse to the police even if you ask us not to:** under Section 19 of the
POCSO Act, any person in India who knows that a child is being sexually abused **must** tell
the police. We are not allowed to check first, and we are not allowed to keep it to
ourselves. We know that is hard to read. We would rather tell you the truth now than
surprise you later.

## 3. For parents and guardians

- **We do not build a profile of your child.** No record of how your child thinks, learns or
  behaves is kept. Teachers see patterns for a whole class section, never an individual, and
  only where at least five students are included.
- **You will be told** if your child says something suggesting they may hurt themselves —
  unless telling you would put them at greater risk.
- **You have the right** to see what data we hold, to withdraw consent, and to have your
  child's data deleted. Email **[GRIEVANCE OFFICER EMAIL]**.
- **One exception we want you to understand.** If a child tells us that someone in their own
  home is hurting them, **we will not contact that home.** We will contact the authorities
  and the child's school instead. This follows guidance in India's national POCSO handbook
  for schools. It exists to protect children, and it is not a judgement about any family.

## 4. For schools

- We ask every school for a **named safeguarding contact** — a counsellor, or the Principal —
  with an email and phone number, re-confirmed each term.
- For serious concerns we contact **our own Designated Child Protection Officer first**,
  within one hour, 24×7. We do not wait for the school, because nobody is at school at 9pm.
- You receive an **anonymised annual report** of flags, escalations and referrals.
- We keep an **unalterable log** of every concern, every review and every notification.
- **Our POCSO duty is our own.** Under Section 19 the obligation falls on the individual
  person who learns of the abuse. Our contract with you does not — and legally cannot —
  transfer it to your staff.

## 7. What this policy does not do

Written down so nobody is misled:

- **We are not a counselling service** and have no clinical competence. We point to people
  who do.
- **We do not monitor your child.** We screen each message for signs of harm as it is sent.
  We do not build a picture of anyone over time.
- **We cannot promise we will catch everything.** Our detection is tested and measured, and
  it is not perfect. **If you are worried about a child, please do not wait for us to notice.**
